To what extent does transitioning from a single-agent to a multi-agent architecture improve the reliability and efficiency of long-horizon LLM research tasks? Conduct a systematic comparison of representative agent frameworks and architectures (e.g., OpenAI Agents, Anthropic Claude-based agents, LangGraph, AutoGen, and CrewAI), focusing on task decomposition, planning, memory management, tool orchestration, inter-agent communication, and error propagation. Analyze empirical evidence from benchmarks involving web research, code generation, and multi-hop reasoning, and compare the approaches using task success rate, factuality, token consumption, latency, and cost per successful task. Finally, identify the specific task characteristics and operating conditions under which multi-agent systems consistently outperform single-agent systems, and distinguish genuine architectural improvements from gains caused by simply allocating more inference-time compute.
